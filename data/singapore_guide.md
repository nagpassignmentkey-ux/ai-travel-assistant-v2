# Singapore Travel Knowledge Base
Source: Wikivoyage & Visit Singapore Official Guide (Metadata: Official Public Tourism Data)
URL: https://www.visitsingapore.com

## 1. Major Attractions & Neighbourhoods
- **Marina Bay**: Home to iconic sights like Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay (Supertree Grove, Flower Dome), and the Singapore Flyer. Best for modern architecture and light shows.
- **Orchard Road**: Singapore's premier shopping district lined with malls, boutiques, and dining options.
- **Chinatown**: Rich cultural hub featuring the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, street markets, and traditional food stalls (Maxwell Food Centre).
- **Little India**: Vibrant neighbourhood filled with textile shops, flower garlands, and historic temples like Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple.
- **Sentosa Island**: Resort island featuring Universal Studios Singapore, S.E.A. Aquarium, and beaches (Palawan, Siloso).

## 2. Local Transportation Guidance
- **MRT (Mass Rapid Transit)**: The cleanest, fastest, and most efficient way to travel across Singapore. Accepts contactless credit/debit cards or EZ-Link stored-value cards.
- **Bases & Taxis**: Extensive public bus network. Taxis and ride-hailing apps (Grab, Gojek) are readily available and affordable.

## 3. Cultural and Practical Tips
- **Language**: English, Mandarin, Malay, and Tamil are official languages. English is widely spoken everywhere.
- **Weather / Climate**: Tropical rainforest climate. Hot and humid year-round with sudden afternoon thunderstorms or heavy rain showers. Umbrella is mandatory.
- **Etiquette**: Strict laws against littering, smoking in prohibited areas, and eating/drinking on the MRT. Tipping is not customary in Singapore.

## 4. Sample 3-Day Itinerary
- **Day 1: Modern Singapore & Bay Area**
  - Morning: Explore Gardens by the Bay (Flower Dome and Cloud Forest).
  - Afternoon: Walk around Marina Bay Sands and shop at The Shoppes.
  - Evening: Watch the Spectra light and water show at Marina Bay, followed by dining at Lau Pa Sat hawker centre.
- **Day 2: Heritage & Cultural Quarters**
  - Morning: Visit Chinatown and the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple. Lunch at Maxwell Food Centre (try Hainanese Chicken Rice).
  - Afternoon: Explore Little India and shop along Mustafa Centre.
  - Evening: Enjoy a bumboat cruise along the Singapore River and Clarke Quay nightlife.
- **Day 3: Family & Leisure (Sentosa)**
  - Morning to Afternoon: Head to Sentosa Island; visit Universal Studios Singapore or S.E.A. Aquarium.
  - Evening: Relax at Siloso Beach and watch the Wings of Time fireworks show.

## 5. Indoor vs Outdoor Alternatives
- **Outdoor Activities**: Gardens by the Bay outdoor gardens, Sentosa beaches, Singapore Botanic Gardens, walking tours.
- **Indoor Alternatives (Rainy Day Options)**: National Gallery Singapore, ArtScience Museum, S.E.A. Aquarium, Jewel Changi Airport (Rain Vortex), shopping malls along Orchard Road.
